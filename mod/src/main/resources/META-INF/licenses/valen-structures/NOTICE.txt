Additional authored HBM prefabs integrated from the same pinned revision:
aircraft_carrier, forest_post, abandoned_arcticstation, radio_house,
broadcasting_tower and factory. Exact original NBTs remain under upstream/hbm.
Office doors use paired native iron doors; narrow rails, switches, anvils and
ordinary furnace/smoker substitutes use current vanilla block states. Original
radio/power/reactor/launch functionality remains unimplemented; no fake working
technology is advertised. Source bomb/mine/radiation triggers do not execute.
Carrier realized source height clamp42 is retained; its fallback Ghoul marker
uses the authored above-water bridge crate. Ordinary storage is empty native
barrels; only original loot markers receive bounded material salvage.
The exact85requested structure types remain additive and separately tracked.
No SCP/anomalous creature content is included.
